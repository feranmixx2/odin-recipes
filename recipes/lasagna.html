Lasagna

A delicious Italian dish made with layers of pasta, sauce, and cheese.

Ingredients:

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Marinara sauce
  • ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese

steps:

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  2. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  3. Spread a thin layer of marinara sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  4. Layer the cooked noodles, ricotta cheese, and marinara sauce.
  5. Top with Mozzarella and Parmesan cheeses.
  6. Bake for 30 minutes or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
