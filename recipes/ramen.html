ramen

A traditional Japanese noodle soup dish.

Ingredients:

  • Ramen noodles
  • Broth
  • Shrimp
  • Soft-boiled egg
  • Green onions

Steps:

  1. Cook the ramen noodles according to package instructions.
  2. Heat the broth in a pot.
  3. Add the cooked noodles and other ingredients to the broth.
  4. Serve hot and enjoy!
