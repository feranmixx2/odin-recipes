Tacos

A popular Mexican dish consisting of a corn or flour tortilla filled with various ingredients.

Ingredients:

  • Tortillas
  • Ground beef
  • Lettuce
  • Tomatoes
  • Cheese
  • Sour cream

Steps:

  1. Cook the ground beef and season with desired spices.
  2. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave.
  3. Fill each tortilla with the cooked beef and other ingredients.
  4. Serve immediately and enjoy!
